Inputs: approved COA, `cog/`, `assess/`.
Outputs:

- BPLAN only: `major_forces.md`, `support_concept.md`, `timeline.md`

Note: TPFDD/TPFDL and full annexes are out of scope for this plugin (Levels 3–4). The skill teaches the considerations but does not produce them.
