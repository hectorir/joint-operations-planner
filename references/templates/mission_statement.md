# Mission Statement

> Doctrinal template — JP 5-0 §5.3.4. Required elements: who, what, when, where, why.

When directed [**when**], <organization or JTF> [**who**], in concert with <partners/coordinating entities> [**who**], <action verb + task> [**what**] to <purpose/desired effect> [**why**] in/at <area> [**where**].

---

## Worked example (illustrative only)

> When directed, Joint Task Force–Cernavia, in concert with USAID and Government of Cernavia, conducts humanitarian assistance and disaster relief operations to mitigate human suffering and restore essential services in the Republic of Cernavia.

## Self-check

- [ ] **Who** — supported organization plus key partners?
- [ ] **What** — single, clearly named action verb? Not a list.
- [ ] **When** — trigger condition stated (or "when directed")?
- [ ] **Where** — operational area named?
- [ ] **Why** — purpose explicit (this is the cognitive anchor for subordinates' intent inference)?
- [ ] No ways or means in the statement (those belong to the COA, not the mission).
