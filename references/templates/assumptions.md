# Planning Assumptions

> Doctrine: JP 5-0 §5.3.1 — assumptions are logical, realistic, and *essential for planning to continue*. Each assumption must have a CCIR (PIR or FFIR) that validates or invalidates it. If invalidated, the plan must be altered.
>
> Discipline: keep the list to the minimum required. Each assumption adds error and bias. Never assume away adversary capabilities or assume unrealistic friendly capabilities.
