## Four possible plan-assessment outcomes (JP 5-0 §11.5)

- **Refine** — orderly process; transmit as FRAGORDs in execution.
- **Adapt** — major modifications driven by changes in strategic direction, OE, or problem.
- **Terminate** — recommend termination when the plan is no longer required.
- **Execute** — review the plan, validate assumptions, issue orders.
