## Consequence scale

| Level | Definition |
|---|---|
| Extreme | Major harm to something of value |
| Major | Significant harm |
| Moderate | Recoverable harm |
| Minor | Minimal harm |

## Risk levels (P × C)

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **High** | Critical objectives unachievable |
| **Significant** | Only most critical objectives achievable |
| **Moderate** | Partial attainment expected |
| **Low** | Full attainment likely |
