# Rubric — COG Analysis Critique

> Used by `/jp-critique` against `cog/friendly.md` and `cog/threat.md`.

## R-COG-01 — COG is named in adversarial context
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.2
- **FAIL:** COG is asserted without naming the adversarial relationship from which it emerges. (Without a defined threat, there may be no enemy COG — if so, document why.)

## R-COG-02 — Critical capabilities are derived from the COG (not asserted)
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.3
- **FAIL:** Critical capabilities are listed without showing how they enable the COG's role.

## R-COG-03 — Critical requirements support specific critical capabilities
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.3
- **FAIL:** Requirements are floating; not tied to a named CC.

## R-COG-04 — Critical vulnerabilities are aspects of critical requirements (not free-floating weaknesses)
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.3
- **FAIL:** "Vulnerabilities" listed that don't trace to a CR. (Common error: listing every weakness in the adversary system, not just the ones that touch the COG.)

## R-COG-05 — No mirror-imaging
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.4
- **WARN:** The threat COG is suspiciously analogous to the friendly COG; or the analysis assumes the adversary will make rational choices by friendly standards.

## R-COG-06 — COG passes the falsification test
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.4
- **FAIL:** The author cannot answer "would defeat/destruction/neutralization of this COG cause the actor to abandon strategic objectives or change COA?" with a clear yes.

## R-COG-07 — Attack-target trade-offs are documented
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.5
- **WARN:** Critical vulnerabilities are listed without scoring criticality / accessibility / redundancy / escalation risk.

## R-COG-08 — Five-question completion check filled in
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §8.6
- **WARN** if the §8.6 questions are blank or cursory.

## R-COG-09 — Decisive points are not confused with COGs
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §7.6, §8.5
- **FAIL:** A decisive point is labeled as the COG, or vice versa. ("Decisive points usually are not COGs, they are the keys to attacking or protecting them.")
