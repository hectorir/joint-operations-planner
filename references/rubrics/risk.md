# Rubric — Risk Articulation Critique

> Used by `/jp-critique` against `01_mission_analysis/risk_assessment_initial.md`, the COA-internal risk discussions, and the CONOPS risk paragraph.

## R-RISK-01 — Both flavors of risk addressed
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §10
- **FAIL** if risk-to-mission and risk-to-force are not separated.

## R-RISK-02 — Probability × consequence rationale stated
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.3.6
- **WARN** if a risk level is asserted without rationale for the P or C value.

## R-RISK-03 — Mitigation options span the three categories
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §10.3
- **WARN** if mitigation only includes one category (reduce likelihood / reduce cost / increase nonorganic support); risk planning should consider all three before resorting to acceptance.

## R-RISK-04 — Residual risk named
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §10.3
- **FAIL** if residual risk is not stated (risk acceptance must be explicit).

## R-RISK-05 — Civilian-leader script applied at the strategic level
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §10.4, §13.6
- **WARN** if strategic risk is communicated in military jargon rather than mission-success terms; **WARN** if quantifications and opportunity-cost statements are absent.

## R-RISK-06 — Risk does not assume away adversary capabilities
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.3.1, §2.5 principle 5
- **FAIL** on language like "the enemy is unlikely to..." without a CCIR backing it.
