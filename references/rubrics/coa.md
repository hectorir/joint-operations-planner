# Rubric — COA Critique

> Used by `/jp-critique` against each `02_coas/coa-NN-*.md`. The five validity tests are the gate; render PASS / WARN / FAIL per test plus completeness checks.

## R-COA-01 — Suitable
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.4.1
- **PASS:** COA accomplishes the mission within commander's guidance — every named task ties to the mission's purpose.
- **FAIL:** COA omits a key task from `01_mission_analysis/tasks.md` (essential), or pursues an unauthorized objective.

## R-COA-02 — Feasible
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.4.1
- **PASS:** Forces, posture, transportation, logistics, and munitions are accomplishable in the time and space described.
- **FAIL:** COA assumes capabilities not in the apportionment or invents future capabilities (violates JP 5-0 §2.5 principle 3).

## R-COA-03 — Acceptable
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.4.1, §10
- **PASS:** Risk articulated for both mission and force, balanced against advantage gained, reconciled with US/USG law, ROE, and policy.
- **FAIL:** Risk discussion absent; or the COA contains an unacceptable risk that is not flagged.
- **WARN:** Risk is articulated but lacks mitigation options.

## R-COA-04 — Distinguishable
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.4.1
- **PASS:** Materially different from other retained COAs in at least one of: focus/main effort, scheme of maneuver, sequencing, primary mechanism, task org, use of reserves.
- **FAIL:** COA is a near-clone of another COA — comparison at Step 5 will be meaningless.

## R-COA-05 — Complete
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.4.1, §5.4
- **PASS:** Answers who/what/where/when/how/why; covers objectives, effects, tasks, forces, deployment, employment, sustainment, time, end-state criteria.
- **FAIL:** Any of these elements missing.

## R-COA-06 — COG and decisive points addressed
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §7.6, §8
- **WARN:** COA does not name the friendly COG it protects, the threat COG it acts on, or the decisive points it acts upon.

## R-COA-07 — Force employment mechanism named
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §9
- **WARN:** COA does not name a defeat / stabilization / competition mechanism, or uses a mechanism that mismatches the context (e.g., "destruction" for a stabilization scenario).

## R-COA-08 — Main effort and supporting efforts designated
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.8.1
- **WARN:** Main effort not designated, or shifts across phases without corresponding shift in support priorities.

## R-COA-09 — Decision points and assessment process referenced
- **Doctrine:** JP 5-0 §5.4 (required components), §11
- **WARN:** No decision points named; no link to the assessment plan in `assess/`.
