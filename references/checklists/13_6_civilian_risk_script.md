# Checklist 13.6 — Risk Discussion Script for Civilian Leaders

> Source: JP 5-0 Practitioner Field Guide §13.6.

- [ ] State the risk in **mission-success terms**, not military jargon.
- [ ] Quantify where you can ("6 months vs. 2 months", "casualties from x to y").
- [ ] Distinguish **risk-to-mission** from **risk-to-force**.
- [ ] Identify the **opportunity cost** — what other plans/contingencies become harder?
- [ ] Offer mitigation options across all three categories: reduce likelihood, reduce cost, augment with nonorganic support.
- [ ] Name the **residual risk** that cannot be eliminated.
