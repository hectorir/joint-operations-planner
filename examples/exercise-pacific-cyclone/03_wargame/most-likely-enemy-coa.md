# Most-Likely Threat COA — Black Reef Syndicate

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

The syndicate moves quickly to exploit the information vacuum during week 1. Brokers seed rumors that the HN government has lost control and that "outsiders" (US/USAID) are running the show. Small fuel and medicine deliveries are made through existing illicit channels at marked-up prices. By week 3, the syndicate has 8–12 brokers operating in affected districts, primarily in coastal small towns where HN police are stretched thin. Direct confrontation with US personnel is avoided.

## Critical events (to wargame against)

1. **D+1 — Information vacuum.** First reports of looting; HN government communications still degraded. Syndicate seeds rumors via SMS and word-of-mouth.
2. **D+5 — Distribution gap.** USAID/HN delivery has not reached interior coastal districts. Syndicate brokers begin distributing fuel at marked-up prices.
3. **D+12 — Broker network expands.** Local small business owners co-opted in 4 districts.
4. **D+25 — Narrative consolidation.** Syndicate-aligned local voices begin appearing in social media as "the people who actually got fuel here first."
5. **D+45 — Influence-buying.** Cash to minor officials for "expedited permits" in recovery construction.
