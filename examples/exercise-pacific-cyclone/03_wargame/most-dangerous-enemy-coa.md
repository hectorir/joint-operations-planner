# Most-Dangerous Threat COA — Black Reef Syndicate + Compound Crisis

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

The syndicate exploits the cyclone aftermath aggressively *and* a secondary natural event compounds the crisis: a magnitude-6.4 earthquake on D+8 damages the partially-restored CER-A airfield and triggers landslides cutting interior roads. The syndicate uses the chaos for direct theft of relief supplies during transit, intimidation of HN police, and a coordinated information campaign claiming the US/USAID response has "failed" and that the syndicate's parallel network is "the only one that works." A single armed clash on D+18 between syndicate elements and HN police produces casualties — including a US military observer wounded by stray fire.

## Critical events (to wargame against)

1. **D+8 — Compound crisis.** Earthquake damages CER-A; landslides isolate interior districts. Syndicate exploits chaos.
2. **D+9 — Direct theft.** Convoy of relief supplies hijacked between Port Cernavia and District 3.
3. **D+12 — Information offensive.** Syndicate-aligned voices push the "US response has failed" narrative.
4. **D+18 — Armed clash.** HN police vs syndicate; US military observer wounded.
5. **D+30 — Pressure on HN government.** Senior HN minister hints publicly that "more direct US help" may be needed — directly threatens the friendly COG.
