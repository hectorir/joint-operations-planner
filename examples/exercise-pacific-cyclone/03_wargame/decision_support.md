# Decision Support — Exercise: Pacific Cyclone

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Decision points

| DP | Trigger | Decision required | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DP-A** | HN-attributed delivery share crosses 70% | Shift JTF main effort from "deliver" to "enable HN sustainment training" | JTF Cdr |
| **DP-B** | PIR-04 indicates broker activity exceeds threshold OR direct threat to US personnel | Force protection posture review by district; advisor presence increase | JTF Cdr coordinated with Embassy |
| **DP-C** | HN govt formally communicates self-sustainment readiness | Initiate drawdown sequence per Phase 3 | JTF Cdr → CCDR for SecDef notification |
| **DP-EQ** | Secondary natural disaster within JOA | Activate B-EQ branch | JTF Cdr |

## Branches

- **B-01 — Accelerated drawdown.** If DP-A triggered before D+30, compress Phase 2 and start Phase 3 early. Drawdown complete by D+75.
- **B-02 — Increased advisory presence.** If DP-B triggers due to syndicate posture, add a small CDF advisory element (no offensive component).
- **B-EQ — Compound crisis.** Activated by secondary natural disaster: hospital ship pulls forward; engineer team re-deploys to airfield; convoy security tightened; PIR-04 LTIOV → 12h.
- **B-CASUALTY — US casualty media response.** If a US service member is wounded/killed in the JOA, response is HN-led-with-US-supporting public framing. Casualty notification per service procedures.

## Sequels

- **S-01 (success).** HN/USAID requests US engineer team return for hurricane-resilient rebuild. Plan as a separate, smaller follow-on activity.
- **S-02 (partial).** Year-long advisory presence requested. Re-enter mission analysis at 6-month review.
- **S-EQ.** Extended engineer presence for compound-crisis rebuild. Force-flow re-planning.

## Refined CCIRs (post-wargame)

- PIR-04 LTIOV reduced from 48h to 24h baseline; 12h during syndicate-spike periods.
- Added FFIR-05: Force protection posture review by district, weekly.
- PIR-01 (USAID lead status) cadence at daily during weeks 1–4 (reflects DP-A sensitivity).

## Shortfalls (carried forward to `06_plan/shortfalls.md`)

- Tactical fuel transport in interior districts.
- HN PAO surge capacity (mitigation: USAID Public Diplomacy augmentation).
- Convoy security organic capacity (mitigation: HN police QRF at JTF call).
