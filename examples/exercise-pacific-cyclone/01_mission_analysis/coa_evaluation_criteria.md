# COA Evaluation Criteria

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario. These criteria drive Step 5 comparison.

| Criterion | Weight | Definition / measurable form |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed to effect** | 0.25 | Days from approval until USAID/DART distribution reaches all affected districts |
| **HN visibility / US footprint** | 0.25 | Composite: HN-attributed delivery share + US public visibility (lower is better for visibility) |
| **Sustainment risk** | 0.15 | Probability of supply chain stress in weeks 4–8 (P × C) |
| **Pacific deterrence impact** | 0.15 | Degree to which forces drawn from steady-state posture; lower is better |
| **Force protection feasibility** | 0.10 | Posture achievable given the criminal-network threat |
| **Drawdown clarity** | 0.10 | Existence of explicit, condition-based drawdown criteria |

## Notes
- All criteria are explicitly weighted; weights sum to 1.00.
- Speed to effect and HN visibility are co-equal at 0.25 — reflects the legitimacy framing in `00_initiation/initial_planning_guidance.md`.
- "Pacific deterrence impact" is a constraint-derived criterion (the SecDef directive not to degrade posture).
