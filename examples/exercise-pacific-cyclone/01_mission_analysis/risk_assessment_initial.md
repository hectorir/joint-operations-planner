## Civilian-leader script (one paragraph)
The mission can deliver USAID-led HADR to all affected districts within 7–10 days at a cost of ~3,000 personnel rotated through Cernavia over 90 days, with a ~40% probability of a sustainment-chain stress event in weeks 4–8 that may require allied ship augmentation. Pacific deterrence posture is preserved; the operation does not degrade steady-state force generation. The principal residual risk is to host-nation legitimacy if the US is seen to lead — mitigated by HN-visible, US-invisible operating posture. The opportunity cost is small: the JTF can be sourced largely from allocated forces without competing with other contingency plans. We accept the residual moderate risk to mission and low risk to force.
