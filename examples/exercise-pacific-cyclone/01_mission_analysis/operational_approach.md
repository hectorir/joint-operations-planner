# Operational Approach — Refined

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario. Refined from `00_initiation/operational_approach.md` after mission analysis. Supersedes the initial sketch.

## Refined problem statement
The principal mission risk is **legitimacy**, not logistics. Every action by JTF-CERNAVIA must ask: does this strengthen or weaken the Government of Cernavia's standing as the recovery's legitimate authority? If a US-led shortcut would deliver supplies 24h faster but degrade HN attribution, we choose the slower path. The transnational criminal network (Black Reef Syndicate) is a derivative threat: it competes for the same legitimacy space and gains foothold only when HN attribution lags or information vacuums persist.

## Refined operational approach (the broad action)
A small, fast-deploying JTF (~3,000) reaches Cernavia within 72 hours of approval, providing engineer, medical, lift, IO, and advisor capacity in support of USAID/DART and the Government of Cernavia. The JTF operates in HN-visible / US-invisible posture throughout. Maritime support (hospital ship + L-class amphib) provides medical depth and aviation reach without growing the ground footprint. Allied ships (Aurelia, Verdania) augment from D+5. The IO cell partners with HN PAO and USAID Public Diplomacy from Day 1, with HN voices leading every public message. The plan transitions to HN/USAID self-sustainment by Day 90 against explicit, condition-based criteria.

## Force employment mechanism
Stabilization — **Support** (JP 5-0 §9.3). DoD enables HN/USAID; does not substitute for them. Indirect approach to the threat COG.

## Decision points and external decisions
- DP-A: USAID/DART transitions from "deliver" to "enable HN sustainment training" — main effort shifts.
- DP-B: Threat COG posture changes — force protection posture review.
- DP-C: HN self-sustainment communicated — drawdown sequence begins.
- DP-EQ: Compound natural disaster — branch B-EQ activated.

## Planning and assessment guidance
- Assessment battle rhythm starts Day 1 (per JP 5-0 §11.1). Weekly Assessment Working Group with USAID/DART representation.
- 72h commander update during weeks 1–4; weekly thereafter.
- Critical MOE: HN-attributed delivery share. Watch the trend, not the snapshot.
