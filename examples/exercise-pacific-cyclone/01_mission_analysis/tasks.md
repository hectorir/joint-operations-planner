# Tasks — Specified, Implied, Essential

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Specified tasks (from WARNORD 26-301)
- Plan a JTF-led HADR response to Cernavia.
- Be prepared to deploy within 72 hours of approval.
- Coordinate force flow with USTRANSCOM.
- Operate in support of USAID/DART; do not assume USG lead.
- Maintain steady-state deterrence posture in the broader region.

## Implied tasks
- Establish JTF-CERNAVIA C2 with reach to USAID/DART and the Embassy.
- Conduct rapid airfield assessment and engineer remediation at CER-A.
- Provide expeditionary medical capability scaled to displaced-person count.
- Establish maritime logistics support (L-class amphib + hospital ship).
- Coordinate with Aurelia and Verdania naval forces operating in support.
- Establish information operations cell with USAID/DART to fill the information vacuum and counter criminal-network narratives — by amplifying HN voices, not US voices.
- Plan force protection without escalating posture (low-vis, mission-tailored).
- Plan transition to HN/USAID self-sustainment with explicit drawdown criteria.

## Essential tasks (must succeed for the directed end state)
1. Enable USAID/DART distribution to reach displaced populations within 7 days.
2. Restore CER-A airfield to capacity sufficient for sustained relief flights within 96 hours.
3. Maintain HN government as the visible lead for recovery throughout the operation.
4. Hand off to HN/USAID self-sustainment within 90 days.

## Notes
- Routine tasks (recon, flank protection, etc.) are not listed per JP 5-0 §5.3.3.
- "Counter the criminal network" is **not** an essential task — it's outside our authorities. The essential task that subsumes the criminal-network problem is #3 (HN visible lead) — denying them the legitimacy vacuum they exploit.
