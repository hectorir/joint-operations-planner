# Constraints and Restraints

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Constraints (must do)
- Operate in support of USAID/DART; preserve USG lead structure.
- Maintain steady-state Indo-Pacific deterrence posture; do not deplete forward forces.
- Be prepared to deploy within 72 hours of approval.
- Coordinate force flow with USTRANSCOM.
- Route public communications through DoS Public Affairs in coordination with USAID.

## Restraints (cannot do)
- No DoD lead role for the response.
- No offensive action against the criminal network outside self-defense.
- No engagement with neighboring states' military forces or vessels beyond standard maritime deconfliction.
- No DoD distribution of relief supplies in HN-visible direct service to populations except where USAID/HN explicitly request and lead the engagement.
