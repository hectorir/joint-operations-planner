# Initial Planning Guidance — Cdr USINDOPACOM (fictional)

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Commander's understanding of the OE
Cyclone BAGEL has caused widespread structural damage along Cernavia's southern coast, including the capital Port Cernavia. Initial reporting from the Embassy and IFRC indicates ~120,000 displaced persons, severe damage to two of three major hospitals, and disruption of fuel and potable water distribution. The single major airfield (CER-A) is damaged but should be operational within 96 hours with engineer support. The port (CER-P) is partially obstructed.

A regional transnational criminal network ("Black Reef Syndicate," fictional) is moving assets toward Cernavia. Reporting suggests they intend to control distribution of black-market fuel, exploit displaced populations for trafficking, and buy local political influence during the recovery.

## Commander's framing of the problem
The presenting problem is "support a HADR response." The deeper problem is **legitimacy**: the Government of Cernavia's continued legitimacy depends on being seen to provide for its people, with US/USAID assistance enabling rather than supplanting it. A misstep that frames the US as the substitute provider of essential services damages both the host nation and US credibility in the region.

The criminal network is a secondary problem — not addressable by direct action under our authorities, but addressable by denying them the conditions they exploit (information vacuums, slow distribution, displaced-population gaps).

## Initial intent
- Move quickly to enable USAID/DART success.
- Operate in the background — visible to those who need to see us, invisible to media when possible.
- Do not degrade Pacific deterrence posture; this is a supporting role, not a force-flow event.
- End state: Cernavia government and USAID can sustain the recovery without DoD presence within 90 days.

## Off the table
- No DoD lead role. DoS / USAID retain USG lead.
- No offensive action against the criminal network. They are a host-nation law enforcement matter.
- No engagement with neighboring states' forces or vessels beyond standard maritime deconfliction.
