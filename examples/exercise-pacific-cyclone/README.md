# Exercise: Pacific Cyclone — Cernavia HADR

> **FICTIONAL UNCLASSIFIED EXERCISE SCENARIO.** All entities, names, locations, and events are invented for training. The Republic of Cernavia is not a real country. Do not use this material in real planning.

## Scenario

A category-5 cyclone (Cyclone "BAGEL") strikes the Republic of Cernavia, a small Pacific island state, causing extensive infrastructure damage, mass displacement, and severe disruption to essential services. Cernavia's government has formally requested US assistance through the Department of State. USINDOPACOM is directed to plan a JTF-led humanitarian assistance and disaster relief (HADR) operation in support of USAID, with potential deterrence implications given the proximity of strategic competitors.

**Plan level:** BPLAN.

## Why this scenario

Selected to exercise the full JPP without geopolitical sensitivity. It stresses:
- Interagency coordination (USAID lead; DoS authorities; supporting mil role)
- Multinational coordination (Cernavia government; allied nations offering ships)
- Stabilization mechanisms (Compel / Control / Influence / Support — JP 5-0 §9.3)
- Credible threat actor (criminal networks exploiting chaos for trafficking and looting)
- Risk-to-mission AND risk-to-force (rotation pressure on Pacific posture)
- Time-critical planning under crisis conditions
