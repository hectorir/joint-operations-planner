# COA-03: maritime-only-supporting

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Sketch
Maritime-only force: hospital ship + L-class amphib + supporting destroyer. No DoD ground footprint in Cernavia. All ground operations executed by HN, USAID, IFRC, MSF, WFP. DoD provides offshore medical, engineer (deployable from ship), and lift via aviation.

## Who
JTF-CERNAVIA (1-star JTF Commander, embarked afloat). JFMCC-led.

## How (summary)
- Phase 0 — Shape: hospital ship sortie from Yokosuka.
- Phase 1 — Maritime Lodgement (D+0 to D+5): ships on station; aviation distribution from ships to HN delivery points.
- Phase 2 — Maritime-Enabled Delivery (D+5 to D+45): HN-led ground; US enables from ships.
- Phase 3 — Hand-off (D+45 to D+75): drawdown.
- Phase 4 — Departure (D+75 to D+90).

Force employment mechanism: **Stabilization — Support** (no direct control or compel posture; offshore enabling).
