# COA-01: small-jtf-fast

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Sketch
A small, fast-deploying JTF (~3,000 personnel) reaches CER-A within 72 hours of approval. Maritime support from a single L-class amphib + hospital ship offshore. JTF-CERNAVIA operates in low-visibility posture, supporting USAID/DART. HN visibly leads delivery; allied ships reduce US lift demand from week 2. Drawdown begins week 6; complete by Day 90.

## Who
JTF-CERNAVIA (1-star JTF Commander). Components: JFLCC (engineer + medical brigade), JFMCC (L-class + hospital ship), JFACC (TPFDD-managed lift). USAID/DART supported.

## What
Conducts HADR operations in support of USAID/DART and the Government of Cernavia.

## Where
Republic of Cernavia (entire JOA), with priority to Port Cernavia and the southern coastal districts.

## When
Deployment within 72 hours of approval; main effort in country D+0 to D+45; drawdown D+45 to D+90.

## Why
To mitigate human suffering and enable HN-led recovery within 90 days.

## How
- Phase 0 — Shape (D-3 to D+0): pre-position engineer + medical packages at Guam; activate JTF C2.
- Phase 1 — Open the Pipe (D+0 to D+5): rapid CER-A restoration; establish maritime logistics; coordinate with HN, USAID/DART, allies.
- Phase 2 — Enable HN Delivery (D+5 to D+30): main effort = HN-visible delivery enabled by US lift, medical, IO.
- Phase 3 — Hand-off (D+30 to D+60): main effort shifts to HN sustainment training; allied ships rotate out; US ground presence draws down.
- Phase 4 — Departure (D+60 to D+90): final transition; HN/USAID self-sustainment confirmed; force redeploys.

Force employment mechanism: **Stabilization — Support** (JP 5-0 §9.3). Indirect approach to the threat COG via denial of the syndicate's critical requirements.

## COG and decisive points focus
- Friendly COG protected by: HN-visible / US-invisible posture; IO cell amplifying HN voices.
- Threat COG (syndicate broker network) acted on indirectly by denying CR-T1, CR-T2, CR-T3 (information vacuum, distribution gaps, cash-for-influence visibility).
- Decisive points: DP-A (HN delivery share crosses 70%); DP-B (criminal posture review); DP-C (HN self-sustainment communicated).

## Decision points and assessment process
DP-A, DP-B, DP-C as defined in `01_mission_analysis/ccirs.md`. Assessment per `assess/`.

## End-state criteria
- HN-attributed delivery share ≥ 80% by D+45.
- HN public communication is the primary source of recovery news in affected districts by D+30.
- DoD presence drawn down to advisory team by D+75; zero by D+90.
