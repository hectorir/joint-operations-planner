# COA-02: large-jtf-deliberate

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Sketch
A larger JTF (~7,500 personnel) deploys deliberately over D+0 to D+10 with a robust two-port lodgement (CER-A and CER-P). Multiple medical and engineer battalions; ground-based distribution capability significantly above HN need. Higher visibility, longer dwell. Drawdown begins D+60; complete by Day 120.

## Who
JTF-CERNAVIA (2-star JTF Commander). Multi-component, full-service representation.

## What / Where / When / Why
HADR operations in Cernavia, deliberate buildup, longer presence. Mission unchanged from §`01_mission_analysis/mission_statement.md`.

## How (summary)
- Phase 0 — Shape (D-7 to D+0): deliberate force flow; staging in Guam and Hawaii.
- Phase 1 — Lodgement (D+0 to D+10): two-port establishment.
- Phase 2 — Sustained Delivery (D+10 to D+60): heavy ground-based distribution, US-visible.
- Phase 3 — Hand-off (D+60 to D+105): drawdown.
- Phase 4 — Departure (D+105 to D+120).

Force employment mechanism: **Stabilization — Control + Support**. Direct delivery posture rather than enabling posture.
