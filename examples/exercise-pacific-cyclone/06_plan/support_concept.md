# Support Concept — BPLAN

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario.

## Concept of logistics support

Two-tier sustainment:

1. **Maritime tier** — L-class amphib + hospital ship offshore. Provides aviation fuel, expeditionary medical (hospital ship), engineer reachback. Sustainment delivered ship-to-shore primarily by aviation and small-craft connectors.

2. **Ground tier** — CER-P (port) for bulk inflow once partial restoration complete (D+3). Allied logistics ships (Aurelia D+5, Verdania D+7) supplement; commercial maritime augmentation if required by D+10.

Last-mile distribution to coastal/interior districts via HNS — Cernavian fishing fleet for water/coastal villages, contracted ground transport where roads are passable.

## Logistics supportability conclusions

- Force flow is achievable within the planning horizon given allocated forces.
- Single-port-of-debarkation risk (CER-A) is mitigated by maritime tier (aviation reach from ships).
- Fuel handling capacity at CER-P stresses at week 4 if allied ships rotate without replacement; B-EQ branch and sequel plans address.

## Key supportability assumptions

- Allied ships arrive on schedule (assumption #4, validated by PIR-03).
- Cernavian fishing fleet remains willing partner for last-mile (validated by HN coordination).
- No force protection escalation that requires hardened sustainment posture (PIR-04 governs).
