# Concept of Operations — Exercise: Pacific Cyclone

> Fictional UNCLASSIFIED training scenario. BPLAN level.

**Plan:** Exercise: Pacific Cyclone
**COA selected:** coa-01 (small-jtf-fast)
**Plan level:** BPLAN
**Date:** 2026-05-07

## 1. Situation
A category-5 cyclone has struck the Republic of Cernavia. The Government of Cernavia has requested USG assistance through DoS. USAID/DART is the USG lead. USINDOPACOM is directed to plan a JTF-led HADR response in support of USAID, without degrading Pacific deterrence posture. Cyclone aftermath has been further complicated by transnational criminal network (Black Reef Syndicate) attempting to exploit the legitimacy vacuum.

## 2. Mission
When directed, Joint Task Force–Cernavia, in concert with USAID/DART and the Government of Cernavia, conducts humanitarian assistance and disaster relief operations in support of the USG response to Cyclone BAGEL to mitigate human suffering and enable host-nation–led recovery in the Republic of Cernavia.

## 3. Commander's intent
- **Purpose:** Enable HN-led recovery; preserve HN legitimacy; demonstrate US partnership without substituting for HN authority.
- **Key tasks:**
  1. Restore CER-A within 96 hours.
  2. Enable USAID/HN delivery to all affected districts within 7 days.
  3. Operate in HN-visible / US-invisible posture throughout.
  4. Hand off to HN/USAID self-sustainment by Day 90.
- **End state:** HN attribution of recovery delivery ≥80% by D+45; HN public communication is the dominant source of recovery news in affected districts by D+30; DoD presence drawn down by D+90.

## 4. Concept of the operation

### 4.1 General
Indirect approach to the threat COG (Black Reef Syndicate broker network) via denial of its critical requirements: information vacuum, distribution gaps, cash-for-influence visibility. Stabilization mechanism: **Support** (per JP 5-0 §9.3) — DoD enables HN/USAID, does not substitute for them.

### 4.2 Phasing
Phases are condition-driven; the date ranges are planning estimates.

| Phase | Name | Ending conditions | Main effort | Supporting efforts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Shape (D-3 to D+0) | JTF C2 stood up; engineer + medical packages staged Guam | C2 stand-up | Lift planning |
| 1 | Open the Pipe (D+0 to D+5) | CER-A operational; maritime logistics in JOA; USAID/DART operating | Engineer & lift | IO, security |
| 2 | Enable HN Delivery (D+5 to D+30) | HN delivery share ≥70% in all districts | HN-enabled delivery (USAID + JTF aviation/medical/IO) | Sustainment, force protection |
| 3 | Hand-off (D+30 to D+60) | HN sustainment training complete; allied ships rotating out | HN sustainment training (advisor team) | Drawdown |
| 4 | Departure (D+60 to D+90) | DoD presence ≤ advisory team; final transition | Closeout | Redeployment |

### 4.3 Sequencing
Combination — Phase 1 elements (C2, engineer, medical, IO) are simultaneous; Phase 1 → Phase 2 transition is sequential and condition-driven.

### 4.4 Main effort and supporting efforts
- Main effort begins as engineer/lift (Phase 1), shifts to HN-enabled delivery (Phase 2), shifts to HN sustainment training (Phase 3). When the main effort shifts, support priorities shift with it.
- Supporting efforts include sustainment, force protection, IO, and assessment throughout.

### 4.5 Decision points and branches/sequels
Per `03_wargame/decision_support.md`. Active branches: B-EQ, B-CASUALTY, B-01, B-02. Active sequels: S-01, S-02, S-EQ.

## 6. Coordinating instructions
- ROE: standing peacetime ROE plus mission-specific RUF for force protection of relief operations.
- CCIRs: per `01_mission_analysis/ccirs.md` (refined post-wargame).
- Battle rhythm: weekly Assessment Working Group; 72h commander update during weeks 1–4, then weekly.
- Reporting: HN-attributed delivery share is the headline FFIR.

## 7. Sustainment concept (BPLAN summary)
See `06_plan/support_concept.md`. Two-tier: maritime sustainment from L-class amphib + hospital ship; ground sustainment from CER-P with allied augmentation. HNS via Cernavian fishing fleet for last-mile in coastal interior. Identified shortfalls (see `shortfalls.md`) are mitigated; none halt the plan.

## 8. Command and signal
- C2 architecture: USINDOPACOM (supported) → JTF-CERNAVIA → JFLCC/JFMCC/JFACC. USTRANSCOM (supporting). USAID/DART supported by JTF.
- Succession of command: JTF Cdr → JFLCC Cdr → JFMCC Cdr.
- Communications: SATCOM primary; HF backup; commercial voice/data tertiary; courier as last resort.

## 9. Risk
**Risk-to-mission:** Moderate. Principal risk is HN legitimacy degradation; mitigated by HN-visible / US-invisible posture and IO partnership. Secondary risk: sustainment chain stress in weeks 4–8; mitigated by HNS for last-mile and 7-day HN delivery buffer.

**Risk-to-force:** Low. Sourcing draws from allocated forces; Pacific steady-state posture preserved.

**Residual:** Moderate residual risk to mission; low residual risk to force. Compound natural disaster addressed via branch B-EQ.
